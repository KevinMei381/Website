Potato Blight

There is also something called the potato blight. It's a disease caused by Phytophthora infestan(Potato late blight fungus) The fungus thrives in humid warm temperaturesand for an infectionb to take hold, a potato leaves msut stay wet for 8 hours. A sign or the blight is water-soaked, dark green-to-brown spots on leaves and stems. On top, white powdery mold appears under leaves in humid weather. Infected potatoes have some brown to purple skin with a reddish-brown dry rot underneath.
