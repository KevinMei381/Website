Examples of potato types
Agria Potato

The Agria potato is a floury potato. It has lots of starch but little moisture. That means it can fall apart being boiled. Their best use is roasted potatoes, chips, fries and mashed potatoes.

Jersey Benne Potato

The Jersey Benne potato is a waxy potato and doesn't fall apart when boiled because of there dense structure. These poatoes have high moisture and little starch. They can be boiled whole and good ingredient into making stew, soup or even a salad.

Rua Potato

The Rua potato is an all-round potato. They have medium starch and moisture. Rua poatoes are half waxy and half floury. Because it's versitile and a all-rounder, it can be used for boiling, mashing and baking.
